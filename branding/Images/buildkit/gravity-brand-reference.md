# GRAVITY — Brand Reference v1.0 (October 2026)
A concept brand for a modern UK retail bank, inspired by the science of black holes. Fictional brand for demonstration purposes.

## 1. The big idea
**The smallest point. The strongest pull.**
At the heart of every galaxy is a singularity — the smallest point in the universe, with the strongest pull. It doesn't chase and it doesn't shout. Everything simply comes together around it and finds its orbit. Gravity is a bank built like that: one point where your whole financial life comes together — and then gets out of your way, so you can look up.

- **Name — GRAVITY.** The force that makes a singularity extraordinary: it pulls things together, holds them steady and keeps everything in orbit. In everyday English it also means seriousness and weight — exactly what you want from the people looking after your money.
- **Signature line (endline under the logo):** Your money, your orbit.
- **Welcome line:** Welcome to the singularity.
- **Campaign platform:** Look up.
- **Purpose:** To help everyone in the UK stop worrying about money — and look up.
- **Positioning:** For people across the UK who want money to feel effortless, Gravity is the bank that brings everything together in one place — with an honest AI companion, real humans on hand and radical transparency — so you can stop staring at your balance and get on with your life.
- **Personality:** Calm · Candid · Curious · Warm. Archetype: the Explorer guided by the Sage — wonder with wisdom.

## 2. Brand pillars (built from the brief's phrases)
1. **One point of gravity** — "Welcome to the singularity." Everything you have in one place: accounts, savings, pots and your other banks (open banking).
2. **Strong pull, zero lock-in** — "Strong gravitational pull." Products good enough to switch for, never a trap: 7-working-day switching, no penalties on easy access.
3. **Your orbit** — "Your money, your orbit" + "Your savings will really take off." You set the rules; we help it grow.
4. **Proof, not promises** — "I want to believe." Radical transparency: fees, service stats, and exactly how AI is used.
5. **The Solar System** — "Solar System." Community and shared money: households, friends, local causes.
6. **Out of this world, never out of touch** — "Out of this world." Delightful design, and real UK-based people 24/7.
Emotional payoff for all six: **Look up.**

## 5. Visual identity
### Logo
- **The Singularity mark** has three parts: the **shadow** (the point where everything comes together), the **lensed halo** (light bending around it — heaviest at the top), and the **accretion band** (crossing in front — the energy that makes it shine). One gradient, lit from the lower left: Accretion Gold → Hot White → Photon Cyan → Horizon Violet.
- **Wordmark:** custom monoline geometric capitals with a crossbar-free Λ — GRΛVITY. Always use the artwork; never type it in a font.
- **Lockups:** horizontal (primary), stacked, symbol only, wordmark only. Versions: colour on dark (primary), colour on light, mono white, mono black.
- **Clear space:** at least the height of the wordmark's "G" on every side.
- **Minimum size:** horizontal lockup 120 px / 30 mm wide; symbol 24 px / 6 mm. Below 32 px use the favicon (micro) mark.
- **Don'ts:** don't recolour or re-order the gradient; don't add outlines, shadows or bevels; don't rotate or stretch; don't retype the wordmark; don't put the colour mark on busy imagery without a dark scrim; don't use the glow version on light backgrounds.

### Typography
- Digital (web and app): **Lexend** for headlines (Lexend Exa for wide-tracked labels) + **Inter** for body and UI (tabular figures for money). Both free under the SIL Open Font Licence.
- Microsoft 365 (PowerPoint, Word, SharePoint, Power Apps, Teams): **Segoe UI** family — Segoe UI Light for display, Segoe UI Semibold for headings, Segoe UI for body.
- Style: sentence-case headlines; eyebrow labels in ALL CAPS with wide tracking (e.g. YOUR MONEY, YOUR ORBIT); tabular numbers; £ amounts with pence (£1,240.50).
- Web type scale: Display 64/72 · H1 48/56 · H2 36/44 · H3 24/32 · Body L 18/28 · Body 16/24 · Caption 13/18 · Eyebrow 12/16 caps +0.2em.

### Graphic devices, motion and sound
- **The Gravity Grid** — a spacetime grid pulled toward a single point. Backgrounds, banners, SharePoint headers, empty states. One well per layout.
- **Orbits** — fine elliptical paths with small "moons" for pots, progress and navigation.
- **The Photon Ring highlight** — one gradient ring or edge to spotlight the single most important element on a screen.
- **Motion:** "Orbit, don't bounce" — smooth eased curves (cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)), 240–600 ms; elements gravitate toward the centre; respect reduced-motion settings.
- **Sonic idea — "The Pull":** a low, warm tone that bends upward into a bright chime (a Doppler shift), about 1.2 seconds.

## 6. Tone of voice
Four principles:
1. **Wonder, in plain English.** Make money feel exciting, never complicated.
2. **Candour at 100%.** Say the true thing first — fees, risks and limits up front.
3. **Warm wit, never at your expense.** A wink, not a joke. Wit goes to zero when life gets hard.
4. **Short, bright, British.** UK English, short sentences, active voice.
Rules:
- Playful name + plain descriptor, always ("Liftoff easy-access saver", "Landing mortgages") — supports FCA Consumer Duty consumer-understanding outcomes.
- Never say "black hole" — in British money-talk it means missing money.
- Never imply money can't come out; "nothing escapes" is only for fraud and data security.
- No film titles, character names or quotes. Avoid "space cadet", "astronomical fees", "Houston, we have a problem".
- Words we love: orbit, pull, lift, light, together, clear, proof, look up. Words we avoid: black hole, sucked in, trapped, crash, vanish, revolutionary, game-changing.
Microcopy:
- Sign-in: "Welcome back to the singularity."
- Payment sent: "Sent. Moving at (almost) the speed of light."
- Savings milestone: "Liftoff! Your Holiday Moon just passed £500."
- Fraud check: "Nothing escapes our attention. Was this you? £249.00 at an online retailer."
- Error: "We've lost signal for a moment. Your money's safe — try again in a few seconds."
- 404 page: "This page drifted past the event horizon. Let's get you back into orbit."
- Loading: "Calculating trajectory…"
- Empty state: "Nothing in orbit yet. Create your first Moon."
- Switch complete: "Strong gravitational pull: your switch is complete."
- Payday nudge (Arc): "Payday has landed. Want me to send £200 into Liftoff?"

## 7. Product universe (names + plain descriptors; all rates and fees illustrative)
Everyday
- **Orbit** — current account. Moons (pots), Lens insights, instant notifications, Boosters round-ups, fee-free card spending abroad.
- **Event Horizon** — premium current account (monthly fee). Black metal card with a photon-ring edge, travel and phone insurance, airport lounge passes, priority Mission Control line, boosted Liftoff rate.
Save
- **Liftoff** — easy-access saver. "Your savings will really take off." Boosters round-ups, no withdrawal penalties.
- **Deep Space** — fixed-term saver (1–5 years). "Send it further. It comes back bigger."
- **Zero-G ISA** — cash ISA. "Tax-free and weightless."
Home
- **Escape Velocity** — first-home saving plan. "Break free of renting."
- **Landing** — mortgages: first-time buyer, remortgage, rate switch. "Bring it home." Includes Soft Landing support if payments get tight.
Borrow
- **Slingshot** — credit-builder credit card. "Use our gravity to build momentum."
Family and community
- **Launchpad** — accounts and cards for 6–17 year olds with parent controls. "Where future stargazers learn money."
- **Solar System** — shared money and community: joint accounts, household Moons, bill splitting, plus Look Up Nights (member stargazing events) and a community fund.
Features (the moons around the products)
- **Moons** pots that orbit your balance · **Boosters** round-ups · **Lens** insights and subscription tracking · **Pull** all your accounts in one view + 7-day switching · **Stardust** cashback rewards · **Gravity Lock** card freeze, gambling block, scam checks · **Arc** AI companion · **Mission Control** 24/7 UK-based people · **Proof** transparency hub and AI register · **Soft Landing** financial-difficulty support.
Protection: eligible deposits protected by the FSCS up to £120,000 per person (limit in force since 1 December 2025).

## 8. Arc — the AI money companion
Personality inspired by the honest, dry-witted robot archetype — its personality, not its looks.
- **Name:** Arc — the arc of light around the singularity, and your financial story arc.
- **Visual:** a glowing arc with a single spark; it sweeps while Arc thinks and completes the circle when a task is done.
- **Personality dials (customer adjustable):** Candour 100% (locked — non-negotiable) · Wit 0–100% (default 40%) · Detail: Brief / Balanced / Deep.
- **Auto-safeguards:** Wit drops to 0% for bereavement, hardship, fraud, complaints and any vulnerability cue; Arc offers a warm handover to Mission Control people.
- **Arc can:** answer balance and spending questions (Lens), move money between Moons, set saving rules, explain products in plain English, run "Is this a scam?" checks, prepare a switch, host the weekly "Look Up" check-in.
- **Arc won't:** give regulated financial advice, push products, guess when unsure, hide fees, or pretend to be human.
- **Sample (illustrative):** Customer: "Can I afford a weekend in Edinburgh next month?" Arc: "Short answer: yes, comfortably. After rent and bills you'll have about £640 left in orbit next month. A typical weekend — train plus two nights — runs £350–£450. Want me to start an 'Edinburgh' Moon and move £100 a week into it?"
- **Colleague agents:** Navigator (policy and product knowledge grounded in The Observatory) · Flight Director (orchestrates Flight Plans workflows).

## 10. Inspiration, not imitation — guardrails
- Use freely: the science (black holes, gravity, orbits, lensing, time), cosmic wonder, the emotional themes (time, family, legacy, looking up), and Gravity's own original imagery.
- Never use: the film's title, character, robot, ship or planet names, dialogue or quotes, stills, posters or screenshots (including the reference images), score or sound design, the film's logo or typeface, or actor likenesses.
- Before any real-world use: trade mark searches (UKIPO/EUIPO, class 36), Companies House, domains and social handles, and an FCA Register name check. "Gravity" is already used by finance businesses outside the UK (e.g. US payments and lending firms).
- Compliance notes: Consumer Duty (clear names with plain descriptors), FSCS disclosures, financial-promotion rules, and AI transparency (Arc always says it is AI).

## 11. Names considered
- **Chosen: Gravity.**
- Alternates worth keeping: **Orrery** (a working model of the solar system, invented in England — heritage plus orbits) · **Lodestar** (the guiding star — trust and direction).
- Ruled out: Horizon (the Post Office Horizon scandal makes it unusable in UK finance) · Black Hole (UK shorthand for missing money) · Singularity / Singular (long, AI-takeover connotations, already used by finance firms overseas — kept as our welcome line instead) · Stellar (crypto network, and too close to the film's title) · Halo (Microsoft gaming franchise) · Lunar / Starling / Atom (existing banks) · Lightyear (existing investing app) · Orbit (a major chewing-gum brand) · Endurance, Gargantua, Tesseract and the robots' names (film IP).
